Hi, everyone!

I'm a research assistant at the University of Rijeka, where I also started my PhD advised by [Lado Kranjčević](https://riteh.uniri.hr/osoba/lado-kranjcevic/), back in January 2025.
My research revolves around artificial intelligence applications in water distribution systems (see my [papers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=l-Mce-4AAAAJ&hl=en) so far).
Before that, I was at Jungheinrich (warehouse logistics automation) and earlier at dSPACE (automotive simulation and automation), where I worked on R&D machine learning projects and software development.

I have a master's degree in engineering from the Faculty of Engineering in Rijeka, where over the course of my studies (2016-2021) my interest leaned heavily toward computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and numerical simulations.
During this period I also developed a massive interest in computer science, specifically artificial intelligence, which led me to self-study this broad topic. 
Along the way, I worked through a bunch of books and courses in machine learning, deep learning, optimization, high-performance computing, statistics, and many more.

I started this blog mainly as a way to write about artificial intelligence, math and computer science topics that I find interesting, but also to express my ideas and meet new interesting people.
I rotate hobbies often, and I've been captivated by a lot of activities, but the ones that stuck for many years are cooking, writing, fitness and boxing.
I also own a [consultancy business](https://marvindigital.hr/) focused on custom algorithm development, machine learning solutions and research support.
